## Brand guidelines (`MOAR_Brand_Guidelines.pdf`)

Key rules, summarised (the PDF is the source of truth):

- **Colours:** MOAR Orange `#FF5900` (primary); Off White `#EDE6DC` and
  Brown `#2D1D17` (secondary). The PDF prints the brown as "#f2d1d17"; its
  RGB (45, 29, 23) is `#2D1D17`.
- **Fonts:** Monument Extended (primary, headlines), DM Sans (secondary, text).
- **Logo:** use only as supplied; no recolouring outside the palette, no drop
  shadow, opacity change, outline, gradient, distortion or rotation. Keep the
  exclusion zone clear. Minimum height 35 px (primary logo incl. "BAR").
- **Approved colourways:** orange logo on brown, white logo on orange,
  orange logo on white/off-white.
- **Iconography:** a set of simple orange icons (bar, shaker, tub, etc.);
  the icon files themselves are not in this folder yet.

Note: the colours inside the logo SVGs (`#FA5310`, `#E7602E`, `#1E1408`,
`#EEE7DB`) differ slightly from the guideline palette above.
